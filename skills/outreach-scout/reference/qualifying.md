# Qualifying a contact

For each candidate, fill in what the page needs, then decide whether they're worth a message.

## What to record

| Field | How to find it |
| --- | --- |
| **Size** | Subscribers or followers, and the country if shown. For YouTube channels, `node scripts/youtube-channel.mjs <channel or video URL>`. Leave it blank rather than guess |
| **Why them** | One sentence naming the piece of work that fits, with its date: "Their October video "Mac Apps You Need" featured two Dock replacements" |
| **Sources** | The link to that work (required), plus any other you relied on |
| **Language** | The language they publish in. Write to them in it if you can write it well |
| **Where to send it** | The best route, then a backup (below) |

## Routes, best first

1. **A business email** they publish for enquiries. YouTube channels often list one on the
   About page; sometimes it's in the channel's links, a linktree or their website. Some are
   only shown after signing in to YouTube ("View email address"): tell the person where to click
   rather than signing in yourself. `youtube-channel.mjs` reports any email that's plainly on
   the page.
2. **The contact form or address on their own site.**
3. **A DM** on the platform they're most active on (X, Instagram, LinkedIn). DMs may be closed:
   always give a backup route.
4. **Newsletters:** reply to an issue, or the address on their about page.
5. **Podcasts:** the show's feedback form or address.
6. **Directories and launch sites:** their submission form (it's a submission, not a pitch).

Never use an email found only behind a login, scraped from somewhere private, or guessed.

## Who's worth it

**Good fit**
- Covered a competitor or the category in the last 12 months.
- Their audience matches who the product is for (the platform, the job, the language).
- Reachable size: roughly 2k–150k for creators; for newsletters and podcasts, any size in a
  close niche.

**Long shots** (one message, may include a code)
- Large audiences (over ~200k), well-known writers, big press.

**Skip**
- Nothing published in the last 12 months.
- Only paid sponsorships (rate cards, "sponsor this video" links and nothing else). Fine to
  note, but the person has to decide whether to pay.
- Audiences built on giveaways or "free stuff".
- Anyone who has asked not to be contacted.

## Groups

Put each contact in one group: best fit, other languages, long shots, directories and launch
sites, newsletters and press. Sort each group best first, and pick **five to start with** across
the groups (best fit first, then the easiest directories).
