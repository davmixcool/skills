# Replies

When someone answers, match their reply to one of these, fill in the brackets, and keep it
short. The page carries the same templates (`replies` in `outreach.json`).

## They said yes, or asked for the licence

First, the person makes a free licence for them in their billing: **a 100%-off discount that
can be used once, named after the contact** (Polar, Paddle, Stripe, Lemon Squeezy and Gumroad
all have one), or the store's own promo codes (App Store promo or offer codes, Google Play promo
codes). Then:

> Thanks [name]! Here's your free licence: go to [WHERE TO REDEEM] and enter the code
> [THEIR CODE] at checkout. It comes to $0, and your key arrives by email.
>
> [ONE LINE ON GETTING STARTED]. Anything odd or missing, tell me and I'll fix it.
>
> If you do end up linking it anywhere, this link lets me see it was you:
> [SITE]/?utm_source=[THEIR NAME]

## They asked about a giveaway

> Happy to! I can give you [NUMBER] licences for your audience. Tell me how you'd like them
> (codes in a DM or a link) and when it goes out, so I'm around to answer questions.

## They asked for payment or a rate

> Thanks for the details. I'm [one person building PRODUCT / a small team] and don't have a
> budget for paid placements right now, so I'll have to pass. The free licence is yours either
> way, and if that changes I'll get in touch.

(Only if the person has decided not to pay. If they might, bring the rate to them first.)

## They asked a question

Answer it in two or three sentences from the product's docs, and link the page. Permissions,
privacy and price are the usual ones.

## They mentioned or reviewed it

> Thank you, [name]! Really appreciate you taking the time. I'll share it, and I'm answering any
> questions in the comments.

Then the person shares it, and answers the comments under it.

## They said it's not for them

> No worries at all, thanks for taking a look. Keep the licence if you like.

## No answer after 7 days (once)

> Hi [name], just making sure this didn't get buried. No pressure at all; the offer stands if
> you ever want to try [PRODUCT]. [DEMO LINK]

Then stop. Mark them "Not now".

## In public threads (Reddit, a launch site's comments)

- "Why not <big alternative>?": say what each is best at, and when the product is the better
  fit. Never knock the alternative.
- "Is it open source / private?": answer plainly, and say how to check (network monitor, docs).
- "Another subscription?": say exactly how the price works.
