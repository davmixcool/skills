# App Idea Scorecard

Score each category from 1 to 5.

## Criteria

### 1. Cost of forgetting
1 = almost no consequence
5 = substantial money, health, legal, asset or opportunity consequence

### 2. Recurrence
1 = one-off
5 = naturally repeats for years

### 3. Domain knowledge advantage
1 = generic alarm is enough
5 = users do not reliably know what/when to remember

### 4. Automatic reminder generation
1 = every reminder must be manually created
5 = app can generate most reminders from a small amount of input

### 5. Willingness to pay
1 = difficult to justify payment
5 = obvious economic or emotional value

### 6. Ad hook strength
1 = hard to explain quickly
5 = problem is obvious in a 5–10 second ad

### 7. App Store search intent
1 = little direct intent
5 = users actively search for the solution category

### 8. Build simplicity
1 = heavy backend/regulatory/integration work
5 = can ship useful V1 largely on-device

### 9. Expansion potential
1 = dead-end utility
5 = natural path to records, recommendations, workflows or services

### 10. Global portability
1 = highly location-specific
5 = useful across many markets with minimal adaptation

## Interpretation

- **42–50:** Strong build candidate
- **35–41:** Test landing page / ads before building
- **28–34:** Needs stronger positioning or a narrower niche
- **Below 28:** Do not build yet

## Example

### Car Maintenance Reminder
- Cost of forgetting: 5
- Recurrence: 5
- Domain knowledge: 5
- Automatic generation: 5
- Willingness to pay: 4
- Ad hook: 5
- Search intent: 4
- Build simplicity: 4
- Expansion: 5

**Total: 47/50**

## Pre-build validation questions

Before coding, answer:

1. What exact responsibility does this app own?
2. What happens if the user forgets?
3. What data is needed to generate reminders?
4. Can the app provide first value in under 60 seconds?
5. Why is Apple Reminders/Google Calendar insufficient?
6. What is the first paid feature?
7. What is the clearest ad hook?
8. What would make the user keep the app installed for a year?
