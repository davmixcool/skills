# X

- **Posts are `platform: "x"`, no community.** 280 characters unless the maker
  has X Premium. Over that, split into a thread: queue each part as its own
  `post`, same day, with "Part 2 of 3: reply to part 1" in `notes`.
- **The extension opens X's composer with the text already in it.** Keep line
  breaks deliberate; X keeps them.
- **What works:**
  - one idea per post;
  - a concrete before and after;
  - a short screen recording (note it in `notes` so the maker attaches it);
  - build-in-public numbers, if they're real.
- **What doesn't:** hashtag lists, "🚀 Excited to announce", threads that are
  one long ad.
- **Links cost reach.** Often better: post the story, then the link as a reply
  (a second `post` item, with "reply to the post above" in `notes`).
- **Replies to other people's posts are `reply` briefs.** The maker writes them.
  X is hostile to reply-guy automation, and accounts get limited for it.
- **Timing:** weekdays, morning US Eastern; one or two posts a day at most.
