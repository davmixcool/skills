# Hacker News

- **Show HN** is for things people can try now. The title is
  `Show HN: <Name> – <what it is>`, factual, under 80 characters, no superlatives.
- **Link post:** set `targetUrl` to the product's page (no UTM tags; HN readers
  notice). The extension opens HN's submit form prefilled.
- **The first comment matters most.** Queue it as a `comment` on the same day,
  with `notes: "post as the first comment on your Show HN; paste its URL here
  after submitting"`. It should cover:
  - who you are, and why you built it;
  - how it works technically;
  - what's hard or unfinished;
  - the price, plainly;
  - what feedback you want.
- **HN readers dislike:** marketing language, asking for upvotes (never do this:
  it gets posts killed), unexplained paywalls, and dodged questions.
- **One Show HN per product,** unless something substantial has changed.
- **The maker must be around** for several hours to answer comments. Put that
  in `notes`.
- **Timing:** weekday mornings US Eastern. Avoid weekends and US holidays.
- **Replies to comments on the thread** are `reply` briefs. The maker writes
  them.
