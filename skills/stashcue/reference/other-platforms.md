# Other platforms

For these, the extension can't fill an editor yet. Queue them as
`platform: "other"` with `targetUrl` set to the page to open. The maker copies
the draft from the side panel.

| Platform | `targetUrl` | What to queue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medium | `https://medium.com/new-story` | Title, subtitle and the article in `body`. Medium labels AI-generated writing and limits its distribution: write it for the maker to revise, and say so in `notes`. |
| Product Hunt | `https://www.producthunt.com/posts/new` | Name, tagline (60 characters), description (260), and the maker's first comment, as separate items or one item with clear headings. Launch-day posts elsewhere link to the Product Hunt page. |
| TikTok | `https://www.tiktok.com/upload` | The caption and hashtags in `body`, and the video idea or shot list in `notes`. The maker records and uploads. |
| YouTube | `https://studio.youtube.com` | Title (under 70 characters), description with links and chapters in `body`. |
| Instagram | `https://www.instagram.com` | Caption in `body`, the visual in `notes`. Links go in the bio, not the caption. |
| Moderator messages | The community's modmail compose URL | The message in `body`: who the maker is, what the product is, that it's paid, and the question "may I post, and with which flair?". |
| Newsletters and creators | `mailto:` or their contact page | A short, specific pitch in `body`, one per recipient, never a mass template. |

Same rules everywhere: disclose, state the price, no invented numbers, and one
text per place.
