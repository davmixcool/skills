# Verdicts

Every community gets one, saved with `save_community`. Pick the strictest one
its rules support; when in doubt, ask the moderators (`modmail-first`).

| Verdict | Meaning | What you queue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `post` | A post of your own is allowed, within limits (flair, format, once a month, karma). | A `post`, with the limits in `notes` |
| `thread-only` | Promotion only in a pinned thread (weekly, monthly or quarterly). | A `comment` with the thread's URL. Threads rotate: check the current one on the day. |
| `modmail-first` | Promotion only with moderator approval, or the rules are unclear. | An `other` item: the modmail, for the maker to send. The post only after they report a yes. |
| `answers-only` | No promotion of any kind, but helpful participation is welcome. | `reply` briefs for threads the maker can genuinely help in, with no product link. |
| `never` | Promotion is banned, often with a permanent ban on the first offence. | Nothing that mentions the product, ever. |

## Reading a community's rules

Look at all of these. The written rules are rarely the whole story.

1. **The rules list:** anything about self-promotion, advertising, "no apps",
   paywalls, open source only, AI-written content, karma or account age.
2. **The sidebar and the submission text:** title formats and required flair.
3. **Pinned posts:** "promotion only in this thread", new rules, bans announced.
4. **The flairs in use:** a "Self Promotion" flair means promotion is expected
   there.
5. **What did well recently:** journey posts with real numbers, or questions? It
   tells you the tone that works.

In `rulesSummary`, quote or closely paraphrase the rules that matter, with their
numbers. In `how`, write the mechanics: flair, title format, the pinned thread's
name, karma or age needed, allowed days.

Set `bansAiText: true` when the rules ban AI-written posts or comments, which is
increasingly common.
