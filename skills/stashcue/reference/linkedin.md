# LinkedIn

- **Posts are `platform: "linkedin"`.** Up to 3,000 characters. The first two
  lines show before "…see more", so they carry the post.
- **What works:**
  - a specific story from building the product: what went wrong, what was
    learned, a real number;
  - short paragraphs, plain words;
  - one question at the end.
- **What doesn't:**
  - hype;
  - "I'm thrilled to announce";
  - emoji bullet lists;
  - a link in the first line;
  - tagging people who weren't involved.
- **Links cut reach.** Many put the link in the first comment instead: queue it
  as a `comment` with `notes: "first comment on the post above"`. The maker
  pastes the post's URL after posting.
- **Comments on other people's posts are `reply` briefs.** The maker writes
  them.
- **Timing:** Tuesday to Thursday, morning in the audience's time zone; two or
  three posts a week at most.
